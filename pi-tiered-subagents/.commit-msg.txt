feat(pi-tiered-subagents): capture child-shaping settings with the launch

Three settings decide what a child is: whether it gets the mid-run
update channel, which extension packages its resource loader must not
resolve, and which prompt-inheritance strategy its provider adopts. All
three used to be read when the child's session was created, so a child
queued behind a concurrency slot adopted whatever the settings said at
that later moment, not what they said when it was asked for.

Now LaunchPreparer captures them with the request, copies them, and the
capture travels with the prepared launch to execution. A run's update
policy comes from the capture rather than the live run config; the
assembler resolves prompt inheritance from the captured per-provider
record rather than the live resolver; and the session factory hands the
captured exclusion list to the loader settings view, with the
composition root falling back to the current setting only for callers
that never went through preparation.

Operational controls (concurrency, retention, interrupt policy) stay
live, as the specification's read-contract table says they should.

Tests pin the capture in four places: preparation keeps the values in
force at request time, execution prefers the capture over a live config
that disagrees, the assembler takes the strategy from the capture, and
the factory forwards the captured exclusion list. 2,284 tests passing.
